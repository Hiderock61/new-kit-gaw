# Stage 39 — First iPhone review pass

Source: two post-Demo-v1 iPhone screenshots reviewed on-device.

## Observed issues

1. Community detail felt visually lonely near the title/description area.
2. The `トピック` section heading did not separate strongly enough from surrounding text.
3. `プロフィールを見る` looked too much like ordinary inline information instead of a navigation control.
4. On profile, `参加コミュニティ` blended into the biography/activity flow instead of reading as a major new section.
5. Click could return selectively where the real-device screenshot showed meaningful spare space.

## Changes in this pass

- Community detail title area now uses the whitespace as a small resident scene with Click.
- Community major section headings use a warm band, stronger type and orange rail.
- The first profile section (`参加コミュニティ`) gets the same strong section-entrance language plus one small Click.
- Person-row navigation hint is rendered as a blue pill/button while the whole row remains the actual tap target.
- Person avatars remain person identity; Click is never substituted for them.

## Rule confirmed by real-device review

Whitespace is not automatically bad, but empty whitespace that carries no hierarchy, atmosphere or interaction cue feels unfinished.

Likewise, a heading must not rely on wording alone. Major hierarchy should be visible through shape, spacing, weight and color before the user reads the label.

This begins the post-Demo-v1 real-device refinement line. It does not reopen or erase the completed Stage 22–38 roadmap.
