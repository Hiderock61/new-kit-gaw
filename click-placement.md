# Click UI placement contract

Source: Click mascot official guide supplied by the user.

- HOME header: movement / scooter with sunglasses
- HOME self card: home standby
- Footprints: sniffing
- Profile: relaxed lying pose
- Community: laptop / watching
- Join decision: hesitant pose
- Validation/rest area: box or sleep

Rules:
- One Click per functional scene, not one per card.
- Click is atmosphere and context, never the tap target.
- Mascot must not cover labels, counts, chevrons, or profile actions.
- Use the half-lidded/nihil baseline expression for ordinary states.
- Sunglasses are for movement or mildly "on" states; bare eyes are for hesitation/soft states.
