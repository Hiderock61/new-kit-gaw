# Stage 40 — Real-device surface palette review

## Trigger

After reviewing iPhone screenshots, the remaining visual problem was not the warm brand direction itself. The screen felt lonely because the major surfaces were too close in hue and luminance, so background, page, section bands and cards blended into one beige field.

Gemini and Copilot were asked to review only the background/surface colors. Both independently converged on the same points:

- keep a warm direction
- reduce yellow/cream dominance
- separate the UI into clear surface layers
- use white or near-white for reading surfaces
- prefer thin borders over heavy shadows

## Stage 40 palette

- Town ground: `#F4F1EB`
- Main page: `#FFFEFC`
- Secondary paper surface: `#F7F5F2`
- Cards: `#FFFFFF`
- Borders: `#E5E2DD`
- Structural section cue: brand blue / `#EAF3FF` soft blue

## Visual grammar

**Cream/greige = town ground**

**Near-white = magazine/page**

**White = information card**

**Blue = structure, route and navigation**

**Orange = footprints, warmth and traces**

## Additional iPhone correction

The community-detail hero still reserved too much vertical space when the mascot was not visually readable. Stage 40 reduces that reserve and uses the known-visible sniff pose in the remaining whitespace.

The Topic heading is also moved out of the warm palette and into the blue structural language. It now gets a small `TOPICS` kicker, a blue left rail and a pale blue surface so it cannot blend into the page background.

The first Profile section uses the same grammar with a `COMMUNITIES` kicker.

## Rule

Do not solve loneliness by making every background darker or by filling every gap with decoration. First make the surface hierarchy legible. Then use Click only where the remaining whitespace has a meaningful role.
