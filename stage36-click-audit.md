# Stage 36 — Click excess audit

## Purpose

Click should feel like a resident of New Kit Gaw, not a repeated sticker. Stage 36 audits the mascot density after Stages 28–33 and removes placements whose meaning is weaker than the information around them.

## Audit result

### HOME

Before audit, HOME could show five product-world mascot beats at once:

1. header movement (`click-move`)
2. footprints (`click-sniff`)
3. movie card pilot
4. town-Chinese card pilot
5. lower explore/rest (`click-rest`)

A sixth Click could also appear in the validation panel.

Decision: return HOME to the established three-beat rhythm:

**move → sniff → rest**

The two community-card pilot mascots are disabled until dedicated movie/food assets exist. The category pictograms remain, so scanability and community identity are preserved.

The validation panel keeps functioning but loses its Click illustration because backstage UI should not contribute to the visible product-world mascot density.

### Profile

Profile previously had two nearby Click moments: a waiting Click below the biography and a peeking Click at the community section.

Decision: remove the biography-area Click and keep the community-peek moment only. A person's profile should visually belong to that person first.

### Footprints

Keep one sniffing Click. This remains the strongest semantic match in the product: traces and presence are exactly what the screen represents.

### Community detail

Keep one small peeking Click. It reads as a resident observing a place and does not take text width.

### Topic detail

Keep active discussions mascot-free. Only the empty-state waiting Click remains.

### Statement detail

Keep the resting Click only at the end of the reading flow. It does not compete with the statement text.

## Final density target

- HOME: 3 main Click encounters
- Profile: 1
- Footprints: 1
- Community detail: 1
- Topic detail: 0 during normal active discussion, 1 only when empty
- Statement detail: 1 at reading end
- Validation/backstage UI: 0 product-world Clicks

## Guardrail

If a future Click placement does not add meaning beyond “there was empty space,” do not add it.

The test is:

**Does the screen meaning explain what Click is doing there without another sentence?**

If not, the correct placement is no Click.
