# Stage 34 — Content density audit

Stage 34 originally called for increasing demo content to approximately:

- people: 10–15
- communities: about 20
- topics: 40–60
- statements/comments: about 100

## Current seeded content

- People: **12**
- Communities: **36**
- Topics: **54**
- Statements/comments: **108**
- Categories: **8** (`文化`, `スポーツ`, `地域`, `食`, `仕事`, `生活`, `ゲーム`, `旅行`)

## Decision

The demo already satisfies or exceeds the Stage 34 density target. Do **not** add filler content just to make the numbers larger.

The next useful test is whether this amount of content produces natural discovery and cross-navigation on a real device:

HOME → Community → Topic → Statement → Profile → another Community

Stage 34 is therefore complete as a **density verification**, with the existing seed data preserved unchanged.

## Guardrail

Future content should be added only when it creates a new route, personality, community flavor, or discovery pattern. Quantity alone is not a reason to add another topic or statement.
